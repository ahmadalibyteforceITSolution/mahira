Featured Recipe: High-Protein Chicken & Chickpea Macaroni Salad

Macros: 420 Calories | 38g Protein | 42g Carbs | 11g Fat | 9g Fiber

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup whole wheat macaroni
  • 1 cup shredded chicken breast
  • 1/2 cup boiled chickpeas
  • Diced cucumber, tomatoes, purple onion
  • 1 tbsp Greek yogurt, 1 tsp olive oil, lemon, Himalayan pink salt
