High Uric Acid Diet: Foods to Avoid (Purines) and Natural Remedies for Gout Pain

High uric acid (hyperuricemia) leads to excruciating gout flare-ups and kidney stones. Learn how dietary adjustments lower uric acid crystals safely.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Uric acid is produced from the biological breakdown of chemical purines.
  • High purine foods to limit: organ meats (kaleji, paya), shellfish, sardines, and high-fructose corn syrup.
  • Tart cherry juice and Vitamin C (500mg daily) are clinically proven to promote urinary uric acid clearance.
  • Drink 3.5 liters of clean water daily to assist renal excretion of urate crystals.

The Surprising Link Between Fructose and Uric Acid

Many people think uric acid is only caused by meat. However, fructose metabolism in the liver directly depletes ATP and produces uric acid as a direct byproduct. Eliminating sugary drinks is often more effective than cutting all protein!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I eat daal (lentils) if I have high uric acid?

Yes! Recent clinical nephrology studies demonstrate that plant-based purines in lentils, beans, and spinach do not trigger gout flares the way organ meats and alcohol do.
