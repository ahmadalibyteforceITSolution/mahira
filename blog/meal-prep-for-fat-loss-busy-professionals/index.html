Sunday Meal Prep for Fat Loss: Cook 5 Days of Healthy Meals in Under 90 Minutes

Failing to plan is planning to fail. When you have delicious, healthy meals ready in your fridge, you never have to resort to greasy office takeout or food delivery apps.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • The "Component Prep" method: batch cook proteins, complex carbs, and roasted veggies separately so you can mix and match daily.
  • Cook 1kg of marinated chicken breast / paneer in the oven in 25 minutes.
  • Boil 4 cups of brown rice/quinoa and 2 cups of chickpeas in pressure cookers simultaneously.
  • Invest in airtight glass food storage containers to maintain freshness and prevent plastic chemicals.

The 90-Minute Sunday Workflow

Minutes 0-15: Chop vegetables and marinate chicken/tofu.
Minutes 15-45: Place chicken and vegetables on baking sheets in oven at 200°C; start rice and lentils on the stove.
Minutes 45-65: Boil 8 eggs for quick breakfasts/snacks; prepare salad dressing in mason jars.
Minutes 65-90: Portion cooked food into glass meal containers, label, and refrigerate!

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do prepped cooked meals stay fresh in the fridge?

Cooked chicken, rice, paneer, and roasted vegetables stay fresh in airtight glass containers for 4 to 5 days at 4°C.
