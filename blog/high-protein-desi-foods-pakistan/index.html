Top 15 High-Protein Desi Foods in Pakistan: Budget-Friendly & Delicious

Hitting your protein target in Pakistan does not require expensive imported protein powders. Here are the top 15 budget-friendly high-protein foods available at your local market.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Chicken breast delivers 31g protein per 100g with virtually zero carbohydrates or fat.
  • Whole desi eggs give 6.5g of complete protein with essential choline and vitamins.
  • Lentils (daal) combined with chickpeas or yogurt maximize amino acid bioavailability.
  • Desi white fish (such as Rohu, Rahu, or Pomfret) provides 20g+ lean protein per 100g.

The Top 15 Protein Powerhouses Ranked

  1. Boneless Chicken Breast: 31g protein per 100g
  2. Lean Beef / Mutton: 26g protein per 100g
  3. Fresh Local Fish: 22g protein per 100g
  4. Egg Whites (3 whites): 11g protein (50 kcal)
  5. Whole Eggs (2 large): 13g protein
  6. Homemade Low-Fat Paneer: 18g protein per 100g
  7. Greek Yogurt / Hung Dahi: 15g protein per 150g
  8. Kala Chana (Black Chickpeas): 15g protein per cup boiled
  9. White Chana (Kabuli Chickpeas): 14g protein per cup boiled
  10. Moong Dal (Yellow Lentils): 14g protein per cup cooked
  11. Masoor Dal (Red Lentils): 13g protein per cup cooked
  12. Soya Chunks: 52g protein per 100g dry
  13. Roasted Bhuna Chana: 20g protein per 100g
  14. Pumpkin & Chia Seeds: 7g protein per 2 tablespoons
  15. Fresh Skimmed Milk: 8g protein per 250ml glass

Frequently Asked Questions

Is red meat bad for fat loss?

Lean cuts of beef or mutton eaten 1-2 times per week provide crucial bioavailable iron, zinc, and B12 without hindering fat loss if kept within your calorie budget.
