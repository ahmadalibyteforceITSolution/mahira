20 Delicious High-Protein Snacks Under 200 Calories for Work, Travel & Cravings

Afternoon cravings can make or break your diet. Keep your metabolism firing and your hunger crushed with these 20 easy high-protein snacks under 200 kcal.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Snacking on 15g+ of protein keeps blood sugar stable and stops 6 PM overeating.
  • Quick grab-and-go options: boiled eggs, roasted chickpeas, Greek yogurt, and edamame.
  • Homemade protein bites beat ultra-processed commercial candy bars.
  • Pair protein with high-volume raw veggies (cucumber, carrots) for maximum fullness.

Top 10 Instant Savory & Sweet High-Protein Snacks

  • 2 Hard-Boiled Eggs with Chaat Masala: 13g protein (140 kcal)
  • 150g Greek Yogurt with Cinnamon & Berries: 15g protein (110 kcal)
  • 50g Roasted Bhuna Chana: 10g protein (180 kcal)
  • 100g Cottage Cheese / Paneer cubes pan-seared: 18g protein (180 kcal)
  • 1 Scoop Whey Protein with Cold Water/Ice: 24g protein (120 kcal)
  • 1 Cup Boiled Edamame pods with sea salt: 17g protein (180 kcal)
  • 1 Can Light Tuna in water with lemon & black pepper: 26g protein (120 kcal)
  • 2 Tablespoons Chia seed pudding with almond milk: 6g protein (130 kcal)
  • 2 Grilled Chicken Tikka Skewers (100g): 22g protein (150 kcal)
  • Sprouted Moong Chaat with lemon & onion: 10g protein (140 kcal)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is snacking necessary to lose weight?

No, snacking is optional. If you feel satisfied with 3 solid meals, you do not need to force snacks. Use them strategically when meals are >5 hours apart.
