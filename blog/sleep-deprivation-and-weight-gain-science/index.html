Why Poor Sleep Makes You Gain Fat: The Leptin, Ghrelin & Cortisol Connection

You can have the most perfect diet plan on earth, but if you sleep 5 hours a night, your fat loss will stall. Discover the neurological and endocrine science of sleep.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • A single week of sleeping 5-6 hours raises hunger hormone Ghrelin by 15% and drops satiety hormone Leptin by 15%.
  • Sleep-deprived individuals consume an average of 350 to 500 extra calories the next day, predominantly from refined carbs.
  • Deep sleep (Slow Wave Sleep) is when your body releases Growth Hormone (GH) for tissue repair and fat burning.
  • Avoid blue light screens 60 minutes before bed to allow natural melatonin synthesis.

The Sleep-Diet Hormonal Axis

When sleep is restricted, your prefrontal cortex (responsible for willpower and decision-making) dampens, while the primitive amygdala (responsible for reward-seeking) becomes hyperactive, making you reach for sugary donuts and high-calorie snacks.

Coach Mahira's Sleep Hygiene Rules for Fat Loss

1. Keep bedroom temperature cool (18-20°C).
2. Stop caffeine consumption after 2:00 PM.
3. Take 300mg Magnesium Glycinate 45 mins before bed.
4. Maintain consistent sleep and wake times even on weekends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I catch up on lost weekday sleep during the weekend?

"Social jetlag" from sleeping in on weekends disrupts your circadian clock further. Prioritize 7-8 hours consistently every single night.
