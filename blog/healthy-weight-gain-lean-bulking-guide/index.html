How to Gain Healthy Weight and Build Curves Without Gaining Belly Fat

Gaining weight healthily is just as challenging as losing it. Learn how to create a clean caloric surplus with nutrient-dense whole foods and progressive resistance training.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Create a moderate 300 to 500 kcal surplus above maintenance to prioritize muscle protein synthesis over fat storage.
  • Do not rely on junk fast food (dirty bulking) which leads to visceral fat and insulin resistance.
  • Utilize high-calorie liquid nutrition: smoothies with oats, peanut butter, banana, milk, and dates.
  • Perform progressive heavy strength training 3-4 times per week to direct calories to muscle tissue.

The Problem with Dirty Bulking

Eating samosas, pastries, and sodas to gain weight creates visceral abdominal fat, high triglycerides, and metabolic dysfunction. A "clean bulk" uses whole foods to add lean tissue and bone density.

Coach Mahira's 600-Calorie Mass Smoothie

Blend 300ml whole milk, 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1 ripe banana, 2 tablespoons pure peanut butter, 2 Medjool dates, and 1 scoop protein powder. Easy to drink, gentle on digestion, and packed with 35g of protein!

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast should I expect to gain weight?

A healthy rate of clean muscle and body recomposition gain is 0.25kg to 0.5kg per week.
