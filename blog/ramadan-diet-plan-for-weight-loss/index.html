Ramadan Diet Plan: How to Lose Weight & Stay Energized During Sehri and Iftar

Many people gain weight during Ramadan due to fried iftars, sugary rooh afza, and heavy midnight eating. Here is how to achieve fat loss and spiritual vitality.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Focus Sehri on complex slow-digesting carbs (oats, whole wheat roti) paired with high protein (eggs, Greek yogurt) for all-day fullness.
  • Break your fast with 1-2 dates and 2 glasses of water; avoid drinking sugary syrups.
  • Air-fry or bake pakoras and samosas instead of deep frying in reusable oil.
  • Distribute 2.5 to 3 liters of water between Iftar and Sehri in steady 250ml intervals.

The Healthy Sehri Formula for Zero Thirst & High Energy

Sehri must provide steady glucose and hydration. Avoid salty, spicy, and heavily oiled gravies that cause intense daytime dehydration. Ideal Sehri: 2 eggs with a small bran roti, 1 bowl of dahi (curd) with chia seeds, and 1 cucumber.

Smart Iftar Strategy

Break fast: 1 Medjool date + water. Pray Maghrib first to give your digestive tract time to wake up. Main meal: Fresh fruit chaat (no added sugar), grilled chicken or chana chaat with lemon, and a balanced dinner 2 hours later.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to workout during Ramadan?

The best times are either 45 minutes before Iftar (light walking/yoga) or 1-2 hours after Iftar (weights/strength training) when you are hydrated.
