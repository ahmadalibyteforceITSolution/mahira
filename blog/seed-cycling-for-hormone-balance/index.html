Seed Cycling for PCOS & Hormonal Balance: Flax, Pumpkin, Sesame & Sunflower Seeds

Seed cycling is a natural naturopathic protocol designed to balance estrogen and progesterone throughout your menstrual cycle. Here is how to do it correctly.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Follicular Phase (Days 1–14): 1 tbsp ground Flaxseeds + 1 tbsp ground Pumpkin seeds daily for healthy estrogen.
  • Luteal Phase (Days 15–28): 1 tbsp ground Sesame seeds + 1 tbsp ground Sunflower seeds daily for progesterone support.
  • Always grind seeds freshly before consumption to maximize nutrient absorption.
  • Rich in zinc, selenium, lignans, and omega-3 essential fatty acids.

Phase 1: Follicular Phase (Day 1 to Day 14)

From the first day of your period until ovulation, your body needs gentle estrogen support. Flaxseeds contain rich phytoestrogenic lignans that bind excess harmful estrogen while supporting healthy levels. Pumpkin seeds are loaded with zinc, essential for progesterone receptor preparation.

Phase 2: Luteal Phase (Day 15 to Day 28)

From ovulation until your next cycle, progesterone must remain elevated to prevent PMS, mood swings, and bloating. Sesame seeds provide lignans and calcium, while sunflower seeds supply vitamin E and selenium to stimulate progesterone production.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my period is completely irregular or absent?

Follow the lunar cycle: start Phase 1 on the New Moon and switch to Phase 2 on the Full Moon until your biological rhythm resets.
