Acid Reflux (GERD) Diet: 9 Foods That Instantly Neutralize Heartburn and Acidity

Relying on daily antacids (PPIs) damages stomach acid and blocks B12 absorption long-term. Discover the root dietary causes of GERD and natural soothing remedies.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Acid reflux is often caused by a malfunctioning Lower Esophageal Sphincter (LES) or increased intra-abdominal pressure from visceral fat.
  • Top soothing foods: oatmeal, bananas, ginger tea, melons, cucumber, and aloe vera juice.
  • Common triggers: deep fried oily foods, raw onions, citrus fruits on empty stomach, mint, chocolate, and caffeine.
  • Never lie down flat within 2.5 to 3 hours after eating dinner.

The Problem with Long-Term Antacid (PPI) Use

While antacids provide temporary symptom relief, chronic use suppresses stomach hydrochloric acid (HCl). This impairs protein breakdown, leads to SIBO, and impairs iron and calcium absorption.

Top Foods to Neutralize Stomach Acid

Oatmeal absorbs stomach acid and creates a protective coating; fresh ginger acts as a natural anti-inflammatory; cold almond milk neutralizes acidity without the heavy saturated fat of whole cow milk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my acid reflux worse at night?

When you lie flat, gravity no longer keeps stomach acid down. Elevate the head of your bed by 6 inches and finish dinner at least 3 hours before sleeping.
