Mindful Eating 101: How Slow Eating Retrains Your Brain to Stop Overeating

It takes approximately 20 minutes for satiety signals to travel from your gut to your brain. If you finish your meal in 5 minutes while scrolling social media, you will always overeat.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Eating while looking at phones/TV disconnects visual feedback, delaying satiety recognition.
  • Put your fork or spoon down between every bite.
  • Engage all 5 senses: notice the aroma, texture, color, and depth of flavors in your food.
  • Rate your hunger on a scale of 1 to 10 before starting and stop eating when you reach a 7 (comfortably satisfied).

The 20-Minute Gut-Brain Lag

When food enters your duodenum, hormones like Cholecystokinin (CCK) and Peptide YY (PYY) are secreted. These hormones travel through the bloodstream to bind receptors in the hypothalamus, signaling that you are full. Fast eating overrides this system, causing severe overeating.

Practical Daily Mindful Eating Habits

Sit at a dedicated dining table without screens. Take smaller bites. Chew each mouthful thoroughly. Appreciate the source and effort that went into your meal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I am forced to eat quick lunches at work?

Even taking a deliberate 3 deep breaths before taking your first bite and chewing properly during a 10-minute break improves digestive enzyme output and satiety.
