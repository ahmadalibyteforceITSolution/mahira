How to Eat at Desi Weddings & Daawats Without Gaining Weight: The Dietitian Strategy

Wedding season in Pakistan and India doesn't have to undo 6 months of hard work. Follow Coach Mahira's 5-step wedding buffet survival protocol.

Key Clinical Takeaways

  • Never arrive at a wedding starving; eat a high-protein snack (2 boiled eggs or Greek yogurt) before leaving home.
  • Fill half your plate with BBQ starters (chicken boti, seekh kebab, fish tikka) and fresh salad.
  • Take 3-4 tablespoons of your favorite rice/curry rather than piling up mountains.
  • The "3-Bite Rule" for luxury desserts: enjoy the taste without consuming 800 extra calories.

The Psychology of Wedding Buffets

Buffets create sensory-specific satiety confusion, encouraging you to taste everything. By committing to a strategy beforehand, you enjoy the wedding delicacies without social awkwardness or post-event guilt.

Buffet Step-by-Step Plate Strategy

Step 1: Drink 2 large glasses of water.
Step 2: Grab cucumber, tomato, and raita.
Step 3: Load grilled BBQ protein (chicken tikka, reshmi kebab).
Step 4: Add 1 small piece of naan or 1/2 cup biryani.
Step 5: Share 1 small bowl of gulab jamun or kheer with family.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I overate at a wedding last night?

Get straight back to your normal healthy routine the next morning. Drink plenty of water and do an extra 20-minute walk. One meal will not ruin your progress.
